# Temperate Japan content bible

Status: approved content direction for ZK-579 on 2026-07-31. This is a
respectful contemporary golf setting, not a historical reconstruction. Final
asset provenance and implementation certification remain separate release gates.

## Identity

Temperate Japan is a contemporary, wooded hillside golf club where carefully
managed course land meets streams, ponds, stone, timber, and seasonal change.
Golf remains central: the setting is understood through route choices, green
approaches, maintenance, club life, and a lived-in modern facility—not through
theme-park shorthand.

## Visual and spatial rules

- Use layered wooded elevation, restrained garden-like transitions near the
  clubhouse, water crossings, stone edges, and contemporary wood/stone
  architecture with clear circulation and service access.
- Let cedar/pine character, maples, flowering deciduous accents, grass, and
  water frame strategic golf rather than turn a hole into a decorative garden.
- Preserve readable fairways, bunkers, tees, greens, staff, golfers, and
  maintenance at all supported camera distances.
- The Vision preview uses an open/darker woodland caption-safe left third and
  a contemporary clubhouse; it excludes overt religious or historical sites.

## Seasonal, care, and Design direction

Spring may be fresh and briefly floral, summer humid and vigorous, autumn
textured with leaf litter and warm color, and winter clear, dormant, or frosty
without declaring the course dead. The economic/care profile is moderate
construction with meaningful mowing, drainage, humidity, leaf-litter, disease,
frost, and recovery tradeoffs.

The Design dock should use clear, literal material and plant labels; show
condition with texture, geometry, and iconography as well as color. Contemporary
clubhouse, bridge, path, seating, drainage, and maintenance pieces need a
quiet, practical vocabulary rather than ornamental imitation.

## Sound and narrative boundaries

Favor wind through trees, stream/pond ambience, maintenance at a distance,
birds, quiet clubhouse activity, and weather. Do not treat the setting as a
generic meditative fantasy or use sacred symbols, religious architecture,
costume, calligraphy, or stereotyped sound cues as decoration.

## Prohibited shortcuts

- Torii, shrine, temple, pagoda, ceremonial-garden, or historical-period
  shorthand used as a visual shortcut.
- Protected real-course likeness, copied architectural identity, or unreviewed
  place/person names.
- A single cherry-blossom palette presented as the whole seasonal identity.

## Approval checklist

- A reviewer reads the setting as contemporary Japanese golf through land,
  facility, seasonal care, and play—not caricature.
- All four seasons maintain golf-first legibility and accessible non-color
  cues.
- Cultural, naming, art-direction, ecology, provenance, and Vision-crop review
  are recorded before the corresponding asset work is certified.

## Recorded decision

Project-owner approval: **yes**—continue with the respectful contemporary
