# Tropical Coastal Resort content bible

Status: approved content direction for ZK-572 on 2026-07-31. It is a
production constraint, not a real-place recreation. Final asset provenance and
implementation certification remain separate release gates.

## Identity

Tropical Coastal Resort is a warm, golf-first destination built along one
visible ocean edge. The course arrives from an ordinary public road, then
opens toward volcanic rock, lagoon water, palms, resort terraces, and long
strategic carries. It is neither an enclosed island nor a generic lush course
with palm trees pasted on top.

## Visual and spatial rules

- Use one ocean-facing horizon or a partial coastal reveal; protect inland
  routing from becoming an ocean-front wallpaper strip.
- Pair dark volcanic stone with pale sand, humid deep greens, turquoise water,
  warm cream plaster, wood, and restrained flowering color.
- Make fairway corridors, green complexes, bunker edges, service routes,
  golfers, staff, and maintenance activity readable at overview scale.
- Use palms, broad-leaf shade, flowering shrubs, and mangrove-like wet-edge
  planting as ecological framing, never as a dense visual wall over play.
- The Vision preview needs a calm caption-safe left third, a visible golf hole,
  and no text or branded/resort likeness.

## Course and operations character

Firm elevated lines, water-adjacent decisions, wind-exposed holes, drainage,
and bunker washout make the coastal setting matter. Water is comparatively
available; humidity, fast growth, storm repair, disease pressure, and drainage
make immaculate turf an operating choice rather than a free visual upgrade.

The Design dock should present golf surfaces, volcanic stone, pale bunker sand,
coastal water, palms, flowering landscape, wet-edge plants, resort paths, and
low-rise hospitality pieces. Labels should explain ecological fit and operating
pressure without pretending any plant is a real-world guarantee.

## Seasonal, sound, and furniture direction

Wet conditions add saturated ground, rapid growth, heavy air, and maintenance
pressure; dry conditions reveal firmer, warmer ground without turning the
course into Desert. Use soft surf, wind through broad foliage, distant birds,
light maintenance, and quiet resort activity. Furniture is practical and
wind-tolerant: shade, terrace seating, low walls, weathered wood, carts, and
service access.

## Prohibited shortcuts

- Ferry arrival, ocean ring, pirate/tiki treatment, fantasy lagoon resort, or
  isolated private-island geography.
- Real-course recreation, recognizable logos, invented cultural costume, or
  runtime art that obscures a playable line.
- Water everywhere, tropical flowers across every fairway, or an economy that
  makes irrigation/repair inconsequential.

## Approval checklist

- At overview and close zoom, a reviewer can identify active golf, public-road
  arrival, one ocean edge, and volcanic/coastal material without confusing it
  with Parkland or Desert.
- Wet and dry expressions remain playable, legible, and distinct.
- The art, audio, material names, Vision crop, and provenance log receive human
  art/design review before the corresponding asset work is certified.

## Recorded decision

Project-owner approval: **golf-first coastal resort**. The ocean, resort, and
volcanic landscape must support playable golf and never replace it as the
primary read.
