## Shipped contract

M40 replaces the flat six-scenario ladder with six data-defined chapters,
three ordered phases per chapter, two state-grounded complications per chapter,
fact-bearing choices, recurring callbacks, playable Player Pro matches, visible
recovery guidance, exact medal predicates, bounded save state, profile-scoped
cross-chapter choices and rewards, and a fact-built epilogue.

The recurring cast is Rowan Vale (advisor/caddie), Mara Voss (rival architect),
Eli Mercer (superintendent), Beatrice Shaw (club captain), Nadia Okafor (town
planner), and Jamie Chen (young regular). Every character has an original
identity, stable gameplay role, four-expression portrait requirement, chapter
continuity, motivations, voice direction, and epilogue outcomes.

The Back Nine requires a build → direct Player Pro round → retained
architecture evidence → redesign/operation loop before its opening match.
Muni Rescue reads live condition, price, staffing, access, and named-regular
facts. Swamp Deal and Links by the Sea read the M39 forecast and drainage state
and finish with playable Mara matches. The Members Club supports all four M39
charters under its fixed fee and culminates in a playable captain's match.
Championship Dream qualifies the property and Player Pro, stages a real
championship event, uses the resumable Player Pro round authority, and produces
victory or honorable-loss endings from the settled result.
